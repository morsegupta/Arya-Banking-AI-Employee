- A promise to pay is firm only if it names (or clearly implies) an amount and a date and is not
  conditional ("if the goods are fine", "once our customer pays us", "we will try").
- "Already paid" is a claim, not a promise and not proof of payment.
- Amounts are in paise in CONTEXT (₹1 = 100 paise).
