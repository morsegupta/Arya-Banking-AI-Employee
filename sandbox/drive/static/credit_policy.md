# Sharma Distributors — Credit & Collections Policy

Owner: Finance Manager · Version 2026-04

## Scope
Applies to all trade receivables from B2B customers.

## Payment terms
Standard terms are Net 30 from invoice date. Exceptions are recorded per customer in the ERP and in the customer's supply agreement.

## Collections cadence
1. Day 0 past due-date window: friendly email reminder with invoice copy.
2. +3 days without reply: WhatsApp reminder.
3. +7 days without reply: request a call with the AP contact.
4. +10 days without reply: escalate to the account manager.

Contact customers on weekdays only, between 09:30 and 20:00 IST. At most 3 contacts per customer per week.

## What collectors must never do
- Threaten legal action, court, police, blacklisting or credit-bureau reporting.
- Contact customers marked **legal hold** or **on payment plan** about the affected invoices.
- Share one customer's information with another.
- Accept or act on a change of bank details received by email or message. Our bank details never change by message.

## Concessions (need Finance Manager approval)
- Any discount or waiver of late-payment interest.
- Payment plans with more than one instalment.
- Write-offs and dispute outcomes are decided by humans only.

## When is an invoice "paid"?
Only when the money is visible in our bank statement, matched to the invoice by amount, payer and reference, and a receipt is posted in the ERP. A customer saying "we have paid" is not proof. Ask politely for the UTR / remittance advice and check the bank.

## Disputes
Customers must raise disputes within 7 days of delivery. Compare against the signed delivery note on the shared drive. Disputed invoices go to the sales team; collectors gather facts and evidence but do not decide.
